Beyond the download

An App Is Judged In The First Ninety Seconds

Onboarding, first render, the moment a list scrolls — that is where users decide whether your product is worth a place on their home screen. We design for those moments first, then build outward to the features that keep people returning in week four.

Every engagement covers the whole lifecycle: product definition, UI/UX, native or cross-platform engineering, QA on real devices, store submission, and the release cadence that follows. You end up with an app you can keep shipping, not one that quietly rots between versions.

  • Performance on real devices

    Tested on mid-tier hardware and poor networks, because that is what most of your users actually have.

  • Platform-native feel

    Navigation, gestures, typography and haptics that match what iOS and Android users already expect.

  • Secure by construction

    Encrypted storage, secure auth flows, certificate pinning and platform privacy requirements handled up front.

  • Built to keep releasing

    Automated builds, staged rollouts, crash reporting and analytics wired in from the very first version.

Our process

How A KeyPels App Gets Built

Seven stages, each with something you can see, click or install at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Assessment

    We start with the problem, the users and the commercial goal — then pressure-test whether an app is genuinely the right answer.

  2. 02

    Scope Definition

    Feature set, platforms, integrations and release plan agreed and written down, with a clear line between version one and later.

  3. 03

    UI/UX Design

    Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype you can put in front of real users before a line of production code exists.

  4. 04

    Development

    Native or cross-platform engineering in two-week cycles, with an installable build on your device at the end of each one.

  5. 05

    Testing & QA

    Automated tests plus manual passes on a real device matrix — different screen sizes, OS versions and network conditions.

  6. 06

    Deployment

    Store listings, assets, review submission, phased rollout and release monitoring across the App Store and Google Play.

  7. 07

    Maintenance & Support

    OS upgrades, crash triage, performance work and a roadmap for the features version two deserves.
