Methodology

Our Approach

How an assistant goes from your existing content to a measurably useful conversation.

  1. 01

    Define The Job

    What the assistant is for, what it must never do and how success will be measured — agreed before any conversation design starts.

  2. 02

    Ground The Knowledge

    Your documentation, policies, product data and past tickets indexed and structured so retrieval returns the right passage rather than a vague match.

  3. 03

    Design The Conversation

    Brand voice, fallback behaviour, escalation triggers and the handful of flows that need to be deterministic rather than generated.

  4. 04

    Connect The Systems

    Order lookups, bookings, quotes and CRM capture wired in, so a conversation can end in a completed action instead of a link.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Deployed behind analytics on the first channel, with human handover live from day one and a transcript attached to every escalation.

  6. 06

    Tune Weekly

    Unanswered questions and escalation reasons reviewed and fixed with real content — the fastest product and content roadmap input you will get.

Common challenges

Ready To Answer Every Visitor Instantly?

What teams worry about before launching an assistant, and how each concern is handled.

  • ?Will it make things up?

    It answers from retrieved content and is instructed to say it does not know and offer handover rather than improvise. Answers can carry citations, and every gap is logged and filled with real content.

  • ?Will it annoy our customers?

    Handover is always one tap away and triggers on request, low confidence, sentiment or topics you flag as human-only. We optimise for resolution, never containment.

  • ?We do not have a knowledge base

    Most clients do not. Website pages, help articles, PDFs, product data and past tickets are usually enough — structuring that material is part of the work.

  • ?Can it actually do anything?

    Yes, when it is connected. Order lookups, bookings, quotes, account changes and CRM capture all happen inside the conversation rather than being handed back as instructions.

  • ?How do we know it is working?

    Resolution rate, escalation rate and reasons, unanswered questions and conversation-to-lead conversion — reported monthly and used to decide what gets fixed next.
