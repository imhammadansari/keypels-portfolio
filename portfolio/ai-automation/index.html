Methodology

Our Approach

How automation work runs — measured first, proven in shadow, then scaled.

  1. 01

    Process Audit

    We shadow the work, measure volume, handling time and error rate, and rank candidates by hours saved against effort to build.

  2. 02

    Design & Guardrails

    Workflow logic, exception paths, approval gates and confidence thresholds agreed and written down before anything is automated.

  3. 03

    Build & Integrate

    The automation is built and connected to your systems, with structured logging and monitoring wired in from the first commit.

  4. 04

    Shadow Run

    It runs alongside the manual process and we compare outputs case by case, so accuracy is proven with your data before anyone relies on it.

  5. 05

    Cutover

    Staged switch-over with a manual fallback available throughout, then a measured comparison against the baseline from the audit.

  6. 06

    Monitor & Extend

    Alerting on failures and silent stalls, change handling when upstream systems move, and the next process on the ranked list.

Common challenges

Ready To Get The Hours Back?

The objections and problems that come up in every automation conversation.

  • ?Not sure what is worth automating?

    The audit is deliberately standalone. You get measured handling times and a ranked opportunity list — and if the numbers do not justify building anything, we will say so.

  • ?Worried AI will make mistakes?

    Nothing ships as an unchecked decision-maker. Extractions carry confidence scores, low-confidence cases route to a human queue, and the shadow-run phase proves accuracy before cutover.

  • ?Do we have to replace our software?

    No. Automation usually sits between the systems you already run. Where a tool has no usable API we build a middleware layer around it rather than replacing it.

  • ?What happens when a system changes?

    Monitoring alerts on failures and on workflows that go silently quiet. On a partner engagement, handling those changes is part of the arrangement.

  • ?Will staff lose control?

    Every workflow has a pause control, a manual override and the ability to reverse or reprocess a run. Automation should remove work from your team, never authority.
